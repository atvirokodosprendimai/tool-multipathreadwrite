## Goal

ADR-211 Decisions 1, 2, 5, 9 and 10 in executable checks and in ADR-206's paid tasks:

- `bench_stage9.py gate` checks one step, a canary or a run directory, with its recorded `bench` exit status, against the registered plans and `pilot.md`, as Decision 9 lists: the receipts the runner's spend stop sums and no deeper one, compared in rounded millionths; a `.started` for every run receipt; a canary's `max_spend_usd` its registered figure and its `init_models` equal to the other canary's of its model and to `pilot.md`'s; every graded run receipt's `init_model` that value; and the step's start (a run's earliest `.started`, a canary's `canary.json` `started`) after its predecessor's clearance as `gates.txt` recorded it, so a replay judges the original times. It exits 0 and prints `gate <model> <arm> cleared <time>` only when the next step may start; that line is what is appended to `gates.txt`.
- `bench_stage9.py review` checks `RESULTS.md` against `counts.json`, the receipts and the kept diffs of the run directories: one row per exposed attempt of M and N, keyed `<model> <arm> <attempt>`, whatever its outcome, `other` included, each quoting the attempt's own kept `.diff` byte for byte, or `unassessable: <reason>` where none was kept, and the oracle and budget reason its receipt gives (the oracle's `missing`, `outside` and `structure` lists, `over_budget`, `timed_out`), the limits that apply, and one of the adjudications the BACKLOG entry registers; no row for an attempt that is not exposed.
- `bench_stage9.py project` reads the smoke's receipts and `plans.json` and exits 1 when an arm's smoke cost per graded attempt times its planned attempts reaches its figure.
- `bench_stage9.py registration --backlog FILE` reads only the Stage 9 entry of the BACKLOG (from its `## Pre-registered: cost per correctly completed edit task` heading to the next `## `) and exits 0 when that entry holds the M-against-A gate `on the same side of 1` and none of the withdrawn conditions: the M-against-N gate, the exposed-and-outside gate, the floor of 3 exposed follow-on tasks.
- ADR-206 T5: depends on ADR-211 T1, T2 and T3; consumes the committed `instructions.txt`; runs the four canaries one at a time, Opus M and N with `--max-spend 0.75` and Sonnet M and N with `--max-spend 0.25`, each one's exit recorded and gated before the next; adds to `pilot.md` `plans sha256`, `smoke plans sha256`, `tasks sha256`, `init model <model>` for each model and `rehearsal` (the path of `rehearsal.md`); runs the smoke from `smoke-plans.json`, each step gated, N with `--keep-scratch-file stage9-exposed.jsonl`, the probe's exit 3 and its stop line kept; runs `count` with the smoke plans on the smoke's output, and `project`. Its fence drops the clause that required a kept file in N, since a real N attempt with no event leaves none and the stub rehearsal (ADR-211 T2) carries the proof that an event reaches `count`, and hashes each N receipt's `kept_scratch_file_sha256`, when present, against its `.kept`; it requires `rehearsal.md`'s date to be the canaries' and its revision and input hashes to be `pilot.md`'s, runs `gate` over every canary and smoke step in order, checks the probe's recorded exit is 3 with the stop's reason, reruns `count` and `project`, and reads `R=${STAGE9_DIR:-docs/bench/stage-9}`.
- ADR-206 T6: the order of ADR-211 Decision 9; each run's exit recorded and `gate` run before the next starts, its clearance line appended to `pilot/gates.txt`; N with `--keep-scratch-file stage9-exposed.jsonl`; the per-arm spend judged by `gate`, not a total of $53. Its fence reruns `gate` on the six steps in order, `review` on `RESULTS.md`, and the counts and report as before, and reads `R` the same way.
- The Stage 9 entry of `docs/adr/BACKLOG.md`: the question narrowed (Decision 1), the one gate, M against A with its interval on the same side of 1 on both models, and what the other comparisons are, with the sentence that registered the M-against-N and exposed-and-outside gates removed (Decision 2), exposure's states (Decision 5) and the three adjudications a review row may carry (`an edit to a line the task did not ask for`, `no such edit`, `unassessable`), the statistic's name (Decision 6), and the second-turn entry's trigger moved to the hazard entry. The three `## From ADR-211` entries are written with the record, not here.
